# FPStudio v0.3.2

The lock screen recognised the enrolled finger three times in fifteen. It now
recognises it every time measured, because the driver waits for a contact the
matcher can actually use instead of reading whatever is on the sensor when the
settle window ends.

## Changes

- Wait for a contact the matcher can use. Settling for three polls, added in
  v0.3.1, fixed *when* the frame is taken; it still handed over whatever was
  there at the end of them. A lock screen is a tap, and a tap is often barely
  on the glass. Paired against the matcher's own verdict over 43 real
  authentication attempts - the driver already logs `Image quality: coverage
  N%` beside every one:

  | contact coverage | accepted |
  |---|---|
  | under 73% | 1 of 11 |
  | 73-78% | 28 of 30 |
  | 79% and over | 0 of 2 |

  Seven of the eleven below the band put fewer than eight tiles of sixteen on
  the sensor, which the acceptance policy refuses before it scores anything at
  all. The ceiling is not a rounding of the floor: across 219 held-out captures
  of the enrolled finger the same split is 196 of 207 inside the band against 3
  of 12 above it, because pressing harder puts more finger on the glass and
  flattens the ridges into it.

  So the driver keeps polling while the contact is outside that band rather
  than spending the attempt on a capture that was never going to match, up to
  1.2 seconds, and then hands over the best of what arrived exactly as before.
  A finger that never lands properly is read as it was.

- Measured afterwards on the lock screen: six attempts, six accepted, every one
  on the first contact, against three of fifteen before (Fisher exact p =
  0.0015). The frame chosen was inside the band all six times, and sharper than
  the first poll in five of them - 80%/19 became 77%/31, 76%/15 became 75%/32.

## What this corrects

v0.3.1's notes said the settle rule, which keeps the frame with the largest
background difference, might be selecting the frame with the least finger on
the sensor. That was inferred from a correlation between metrics rather than
measured against acceptance. Measured directly, the difference runs the right
way: the lighter press is the one that keeps the ridges. The rule is unchanged.
What was missing was a floor under it, and a ceiling over it.

## Upgrade notes

- The driver patch is the fix. A package rebuilt without
  `0024-goodix55x4-wait-for-a-usable-contact.patch` keeps the old behaviour,
  and `--refresh-experimental-auth` is enough to deploy it: the gallery,
  enrolment and PAM stacks are untouched.
- Accepted scores sit closer to the floor than the best historical runs -
  0.873 to 0.963 against a 0.860 threshold, with other fingers measured at
  0.844. Every attempt passed, but the margin is 0.029. Re-enrolling widens it.
- Biometric matching remains experimental and is not validated as a false-accept
  rate. The separations quoted here were measured on one person's fingers.
